## Introduction

**ES/YUSO** is an online electric charging station search service, giving you access to very fast electric charging stations. It offers an interactive map that lets you find stations around a chosen location, by searching for a city or browsing directly on the map.

The service is designed to:

- simplify your search for very fast charging stations: during a trip, medium and fast stations can take more than two hours, while very fast stations can charge in less than 45 minutes;
- help you find accurate, up-to-date information;
- make it easier to create routes with Google Maps.

## Access ES/YUSO

Open [ES/YUSO](https://ethicseido.com/Yuso/Yuso) in your preferred browser (Chrome, Brave, Edge, Firefox, and more).

## Get started

Enter the city where you want to find a very fast charging station in the search box at the top centre of the page.

ES/YUSO is here to support your electric-vehicle journeys on very fast charging networks. Feel free to contact us with questions or feedback.

Happy electric mobility! ⚡

## Send a charging station to Google Maps

To create a route in Google Maps, select a station on the ES/YUSO map, then choose **Send to Google Map App**.

## Use voice search

Select the microphone icon <a href="https://ethicseido.com/Yuso/Yuso"><img src="../assets/MCwA098x96.png" alt="Voice search icon" /></a> in the ES/YUSO search bar. Grant microphone access if prompted, then speak: your words will appear in the search field.
